How to play

Tap rocks to cut them. Ore drops when a rock shatters — haulers then carry those chips to the REFINERY pad. Chip Harvest (Tap tree) is a chance to leak extra ore as a rock takes damage, and starts at 0%. Probes cost credits to launch and do not respawn. After a wall bounce they glow gold — Bank Shot (Fleet) turns that glow into bonus damage. Probe crit chance and crit damage sit on their own Ballistics tree, separate from pick crits. Auto-Launch is a mid-game Fleet unlock and still pays the launch fee.
